Fan Support, Mission Rules, Schedules, and Official Links

Prologue: Voting & Fan Support Rules

Support your picks! Based on popularity votes, top Zodiac trainees will be selected to launch their exclusive personal accounts and receive long-term promotional resources!

1. Voting Channel

After each official episode drops, the official account 【12 Stars】 will open voting in the video's comment section.

2. How to Vote

  1. Go to the comment section of the latest official 【12 Stars】 video and find the official pinned comment for your favorite Zodiac sign.
  2. Simply "Like" that comment! The number of Likes equals the popularity votes for that Zodiac sign. Votes determine the stage Center and screen time allocation.

3. Additional Information

  • Only likes on the official comments count as valid votes. Fan-made comments do not count toward the final tally.
  • The voting period is strictly based on the pinned rules in each video. Once the period ends, voting locks and new likes are not accumulated.
  • The official 【12 Stars】 account reserves the right of final interpretation.

Core Declarations & Clarifications

1. Key Voting Rules, Clarified!

  • 1. Fixed Rule: One trainee from each of the 4 Elements (Fire, Earth, Air, Water) will ultimately debut. (This guarantees every element gets a complete storyline).
  • 2. Votes reset per mission. The 1st Mission votes only apply to the 1st Mission. The 2nd and 3rd Missions will have brand new voting channels! This ensures that trainees with temporarily lower votes aren't doomed to be background characters forever.
  • 3. A single vote only decides the Center for *that* specific stage, not the overarching plot! Initial pre-produced episodes have already been finalized. Your votes shape future content.
  • 4. We are constantly optimizing the voting channels. If you have fair and user-friendly ideas, let's discuss them!

2. Important Notice: 100% FREE

Let’s be clear: This is an AI-generated virtual idol survival series! We will NEVER ask you for a single cent. There are no top-ups, paid voting, or paid screen-time unlocks. Please stay vigilant and protect your wallets from scammers! Voting exists solely for interaction and to shape the story you want to see.

3. About Screen Time & Storylines

Early episodes feature pre-produced build-up storylines. We release them to gather your feedback and discussions, which then dictate the 1st Mission and beyond. We do not intentionally cut any trainee's screen time.

4. Stages, Styling & Vocals

1. Every trainee gets new styling tailored to each stage. Initial concepts leaned into Zodiac stereotypes just to help you identify them instantly. Don't worry, styles will evolve!

2. All 1st Mission songs are 100% original! Vocals are fine-tuned real human voices. There's no intentional vocal nerfing or boosting—only differences in vocal tone, not skill.

3. Wondering why judges critique seemingly good singing? Due to AI limitations, we can't create massive skill gaps. Critiques exist purely to drive the underdog/growth storyline, not to degrade any trainee.

4. We treat all trainees equally. There is no favoritism. Every storyline and screen time allocation is meticulously crafted for the overall show's pacing.

5. Fan Creations & Secondary Works

We highly welcome fan creations! Tag us (@) and we will absolutely watch them! However, malicious slander or copyright infringement (stealing images/original songs) is strictly prohibited.

Final Thoughts

The original intention behind this series was to bring back the pure joy of watching idol survival shows. Feel free to leave comments regarding storylines, stages, and trainees. We read every single one and will adjust accordingly. Please keep it respectful—we just want everyone to have a fun and relaxing time watching!

Trainee Profiles: The 4 Elements

The 12 trainees are divided into four main camps based on their Zodiac elements, each coming with a unique "Stage Persona" and character arc:

Fire Element (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)

Stage Mood Makers | Explosive Power

Born with a "passionate, confident, hates-losing" storyline. They are the stage kings/queens who grab your attention immediately. Often taking on Main Rap, High Note Vocal, or Center roles. Direct and outspoken, they are the main source of dramatic conflict and highlights.

Earth Element (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)

The Solid Foundation | Practice Room Monsters

Born with a "cautious, detail-obsessed, All-Rounder ACE" storyline. Extremely self-disciplined, they often turn into "demon instructors" during practice, tolerating zero mistakes. Usually serving as Main Dancers or Team Leaders, they are the anchor for stage quality.

Air Element (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)

Charm Machines | Concept Chameleons

Born with a "vibrant, versatile, social butterfly" storyline. Natural-born idols with an impeccable sense for finding the camera. They effortlessly pull off the weirdest, most unique concepts and outfits. Mostly serving as Visuals or versatile Main Dancers, always delivering surprises.

Water Element (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)

Emotion Stealers | Masters of Empathy

Born with a "delicate, sensitive, stunning vocal tone" storyline. They are the stage's emotional transmitters; just standing still and singing is enough to make audiences cry. Often taking Main Vocal or Emotion roles, their angsty arcs make them the trainees fans want to "protect" the most.

Round 2 (2nd Mission): Best Stage & Individual Rules

✦ Basic Mechanics ✦

  • 1. Grouping: 4 stages total, 3 members per stage (A, B, C, D).
  • 2. Battles: Stage A vs Stage B, Stage C vs Stage D.
  • 3. Battle Benefits: Winning stages earn benefits. Center earns +5,000 votes, other members earn +2,000 votes. (Losing teams get 0).
  • 4. Best Stage Voting: Audiences vote for the "Best Stage" out of the 4. The top two ranking members from the "Best Stage" automatically become Centers for the 2nd Mission!
  • 5. Best Performer: In the remaining 3 stages, the top-voted member in each stage becomes the "Best Performer". These three will compete for the final Center spot in the 2nd Mission!

Round 3 (3rd Mission): Voting & Song Selection

1. 3rd Round Voting Rules

The 2nd Mission consists of 3 stages: Vocal, Rap, and Dance. Votes will determine the Best Stage among these three.

Benefits & Penalties

Best Stage Center Privilege (+7,000 Votes)

Gains the right to lock in their song choice for the 3rd Mission and becomes the sole Center for that stage.

Lowest Stage Penalty

The last-place trainee in the lowest-ranked stage immediately loses their song selection rights for the 3rd Mission.

2. 3rd Mission Song Selection (By Rank)

There are 3 original songs with 4 trainees per stage. Fans will vote to decide the trainees' stages.
