Immunology & mRNA Design Pipeline 01

ImmunoPharm-VaxScreen

Frozen ESM-2 epitope screening under homology-cluster splits, peptide hemolysis/cytotoxicity gating, and mRNA CAI/MFE profiling.

Colab Open in Colab GitHub Repo

Anti-Data-Leakage Controls

  • Homology-Cluster Partitioning (≤30% ID): Clusters pathogen antigens by sequence identity before splitting so homologous epitopes never cross train/test boundaries.
  • Frozen ESM-2 & Fold-Scoped Scaling: Extracts fixed PLM embeddings without test-time adaptation; feature scalers and thresholds are fit strictly on training folds.
  • Out-of-Fold Safety & mRNA Gating: Applies peptide hemolysis/cytotoxicity gates and mRNA Codon Adaptation Index (CAI) / MFE profiling strictly post-split.

Chemoinformatics & ADMET Pipeline 02

PharmADMET-ScaffoldBench

Bemis-Murcko scaffold-split liver microsomal stability benchmark with RDKit CYP450 metabolic soft-spot attribution.

Colab Open in Colab GitHub Repo

Anti-Data-Leakage Controls

  • Deterministic Bemis-Murcko Scaffold Split: Locks all congeneric analogs sharing a ring-linker scaffold into one partition to measure genuine out-of-scaffold generalization.
  • Pre-Split RDKit Standardization & Deduplication: Executes salt stripping, charge neutralization, canonical tautomerization, and replicate aggregation before split assignment.
  • Inner-Fold Descriptor Filtering & Attribution: Fits Morgan fingerprint variance filters and LightGBM early-stopping strictly inside training folds prior to CYP450 soft-spot attribution.
