Anti-Data-Leakage Controls

  • • Sequence-Homology Cluster Partitioning (≤30% ID): Groups antigens by sequence similarity prior to train/validation/test assignment so homologous pathogen epitopes never span across splits.
  • • Frozen Pretrained ESM-2 & Fold-Scoped Scaling: Extracts fixed PLM representations without test-set adaptation; feature normalizers and classification thresholds are fit strictly on training folds.
  • • Post-Split Toxicity & mRNA Construct Gating: Evaluates downstream peptide hemolysis/cytotoxicity filters and mRNA Codon Adaptation Index (CAI) / Minimum Free Energy (MFE) profiling strictly out-of-fold.

PharmADMET-ScaffoldBench

Anti-Data-Leakage Controls

  • • Deterministic Bemis-Murcko Scaffold Grouping: Assigns all congeneric analogs sharing an identical ring-linker core to the same partition to benchmark true out-of-scaffold generalization.
  • • Pre-Split RDKit Standardization & Deduplication: Performs salt stripping, charge neutralization, canonical tautomerization, and replicate assay aggregation prior to scaffold splitting to prevent cross-split twins.
  • • Inner-Fold Descriptor Filtering & Attribution: Fits Morgan fingerprint variance filters, feature scalers, and LightGBM early-stopping strictly within training folds prior to held-out CYP450 soft-spot attribution.
