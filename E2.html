Section 05

Limitations, "Data Cliff" & Future Coexistence

Examining the sharp drop in engagement after novelty wore off, the core limitations of AI characters, and the emerging human-AI collaborative framework.

The "Data Cliff": Mid-August Engagement Drop

Engagement Decay

Official video likes plummeted from a peak of ~700k down to ~50k during the "Summer Sports Day" episode, revealing the temporary nature of novelty traffic.

✦ June-July: Viral Peak Period (700k+) ✦ Mid-August: Novelty Exhaustion (50k)

1. Shallow Labeling & Low Retention

"Zodiac Signs" work well for fast top-of-funnel conversion, but like MBTI or blood types, they are consumed casually. They lack the depth needed to build long-term core fandom loyalty.

2. Absence of Human "Hesitation" & Empathy

AI creates flawless visuals but lacks raw human imperfection, genuine reactions, and vulnerability. The moment fans realize dramatic arcs are pre-programmed scripts, emotional immersion drops.

3. Post-Debut Career Disconnection

Real survival shows transition winners into physical music shows, concerts, and variety appearances. AI survival shows currently lack a tangible real-world career path post-debut.

Entertainment Industry Perspective (Yuehua Ent CEO Du Hua)
"AI cannot replace real human idols; they will exist in symbiosis. In the future, core competitiveness will rest on irreplaceable human warmth, IP creation, and artistic creativity."
Literary & Creative Perspective (Author Kim Ae-ran)
"There is one thing humans have that AI completely lacks: 'hesitation'. The magic of variety content lies in human imperfection, unexpected choices, and genuine hesitation."

Future Outlook: A Human-AI Collaborative Content Ecosystem

Viewers are evolving from passive observers into active co-creators guiding AI character arcs. AI serves as a hyper-efficient tool, but the creative spark, emotional connection, and strategic vision will forever remain human.
